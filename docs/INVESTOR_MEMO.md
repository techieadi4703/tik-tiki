# Tiktiki: Executive Brief & Investor Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Company Name**: Tiktiki (JourneyOS)
- **Sector**: Consumer Travel Intelligence & Intercity Mobility
- **Core Hypothesis**: Indian travel planning is broken because incumbents (MakeMyTrip, redRail, ixigo) are captive retail storefronts that monetize inventory transactions rather than optimizing the traveler's door-to-door journey.
- **The Solution**: An independent, multi-modal travel intelligence engine that discovers hidden intermediate seat quotas, evaluates connecting routes with delay-risk calculations, and snipes opening seats in real time.

## 3. Unit Economics & Monetization Mechanics

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Strategy: The Zero-CAC Flywheel
- **Organic SEO Route Moat**: Programmatic generation of 5,000 route pages (`/trains/itarsi-to-prayagraj`) targeting long-tail queries where trains are waitlisted.
- **Viral Referral Loop**: Users get 3 free WhatsApp sniping alerts; sharing an invite with a travel companion unlocks 5 additional watches.

### Revenue Breakdown:
1. **Tiktiki Pro (Subscription)**: ₹99/month (~$1.20 USD).
   - Target: 10,000 active subscribers by Month 12 = **₹11.88 Lakh MRR ($14,000/mo)**.
2. **Affiliate Commissions**:
   - Intercity Buses: ₹60 average commission per redirected booking.
   - Transit Hotels: ₹250 average commission per booking.
